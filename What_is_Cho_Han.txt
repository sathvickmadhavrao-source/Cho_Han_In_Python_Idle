Cho-Han is a Japanese game played back in the Edo-Period times(1603-1868). A dealer shakes two six-sided dice inside a bamboo cup or a bowl, then slams it on the floor. The players gather around and bet whether it is even or odd. The dealer lifts a cup, if the sum of the number on each dice is even, then those who bet even win, if it is odd, then those who bet odd win.
